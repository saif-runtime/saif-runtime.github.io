Saif comes with out-of-the-box multiplayer support. It uses [San Andreas Multiplayer](https://www.sa-mp.mp/) (SA:MP), an established multiplayer mod for GTA SA, natively on your Mac to connect you to thousands of players. This means you can play cross-platform with other (Windows) players on existing SA:MP servers.

## Accessing multiplayer
You can access the Saif multiplayer launcher by clicking on the "Multiplayer" button after launching Saif.
